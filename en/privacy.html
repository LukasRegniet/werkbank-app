Privacy Policy

For the iOS app “Werkbank” · Last updated: October 5, 2026 · Deutsch

In short: Werkbank does not collect any data. The app needs no account, works without the internet, shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking tools.

What the app stores

The app stores the following only on your device:

  • Progress in projects (current step, checked-off materials, running timers)
  • Photos you add to the project album
  • Whether the introduction has been shown
  • Optionally one or two of your children’s first names (see “Children’s names”)

This data never leaves your device. We have no access to it. Deleting the app deletes it. If you use iCloud Backup, Apple backs it up as part of your device backup; Apple’s privacy policy applies to that.

Permissions

  • Camera and photos: only when you take or choose a photo for the album. The app does not access your photos otherwise.
  • Camera for “See it for real” (AR): When you place a model on a table or the floor, Werkbank temporarily processes the camera image on your device to detect surfaces and light. Werkbank does not store or transmit these images.
  • Notifications: only when you start a drying timer. The reminder is created locally on your device; no server is used. While a timer is running, it can also appear as a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island (project name, step, remaining time; no children’s names).

External links

Project pages and “About Werkbank” link to the YouTube videos that inspired the projects. These links are behind a parent gate. When you open a link, you leave the app and the privacy policy of that provider applies (e.g. Google/YouTube).

Under “What do you have?” there are also store links (Amazon, and in Germany and Austria a hardware store of your choice: Hornbach, OBI or toom), also behind the parent gate. They open the store’s search in Safari (or in the store’s app, if installed) with the search term for the missing item; the store receives that search term and the usual connection data of your device, Werkbank itself transmits nothing. From then on, the store’s privacy rules apply. Should Werkbank take part in the Amazon Associates program, this will be stated in the app under “About Werkbank”; even then the app does not learn what you buy.

Children’s names

Under “About Werkbank” and on every project page you can optionally enter one or two first names so the app can say whose turn it is. These names are stored only locally on your device (in the app settings), never sent to us or third parties and not used for anything else. You can delete or change them in the app at any time; deleting the app removes them too. A nickname or a letter is enough.

Children

Werkbank is made for parents who build things together with their children. The app collects no personal data, including from children. The optional children’s names stay on the device (see above).
