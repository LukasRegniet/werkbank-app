Frequently asked questions

Why do the projects say “Draft”?

Every set of instructions is only marked as checked after we have actually built it. Until then the label stays: measurements and order have been reviewed carefully but not yet confirmed on a real build.

The instructions suddenly start at step 1 again.

When we revise a set of instructions, the saved step numbers no longer match. The app then resets that one project to the beginning and tells you once. Whatever you have already built stays, of course; just skip ahead to where you were. Checked-off materials are reset because measurements may have changed.

The timer doesn’t notify me.

Notifications must be allowed for Werkbank in the iOS Settings. The reminder is created locally on your device. Time passed does not mean dry: an adult checks with a fingernail.

The photo could not be saved.

Usually the storage is full. Free up some space and tap “Try again”; the photo stays queued in the app until you leave the finish screen.

How do I delete the children’s names or the progress?

Children’s names: clear the fields under “About Werkbank”. Progress of a project: “Start over” on the project page. Everything: delete the app. There is no account and no data on our side.

Safety

Tools can cause injuries. An adult does all sawing, drilling and cutting and is responsible. Children only work under supervision and wear safety glasses. The instructions come without warranty; you build at your own risk.
